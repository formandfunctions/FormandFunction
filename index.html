03 / Workflow

Your project,
from planning
to launch.

You review the direction before we move on. Each stage has a practical outcome.

  1. 01

    Discovery & planning.

    We talk through your business, customers, content, and the job the website needs to do. Then we set out the scope.

    Project brief & page plan
  2. 02

    Design & review.

    We develop the layout and visual direction. You review it, we discuss the details, and we make the changes.

    Design for your approval
  3. 03

    Development & testing.

    We turn the design into a working site and check the key pages, mobile layouts, and user journeys.

    Working preview for final review
  4. 04

    Launch & support.

    We take the site live, walk you through managing it, and agree any ongoing support you need.

    Live website & practical handover
Before we start
Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We’ll look at what is worth keeping and what needs changing, then agree whether an update or a rebuild makes more sense.

How do you price a project?

We work from the scope: the pages, content, functionality, and services you need. Those details go into a proposal, so you know what the project includes.

Can I update the website myself?

If that’s important to you, we can include a suitable content management system and show you how to use it during handover.
